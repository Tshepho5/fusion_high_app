## What is collected

For a school-registration application, Geleza SA collects the school name, EMIS number, province, district, circuit, address, school contact email and phone, curriculum, grade range, streams, languages, subjects, motto, colours, selected modules, and logo.

It also collects the principal’s first name, surname, South African ID number, email address, phone number, and optional SACE number.

For a learner admission, Geleza SA collects the learner’s name, South African ID number, address, grade, stream, and home language, and the parent or guardian’s name, relationship, ID number, phone, email, and address, together with the documents the applicant uploads.

## Why it is collected

School-registration information is collected so an authorised Geleza SA platform superadmin can review who is asking to register the school and whether that person may act for it. The principal’s ID number is kept for that review. It does not by itself prove identity or authority.

Learner-admission information is collected so the selected school can consider the application, record a fee choice, and, after the school’s own decision, enrol the learner. A school fee is not taken when the application is submitted.

## Who can see an application

School-registration applications are opened by platform superadmins. Another school’s users do not receive that application. A pending application does not create a school or a principal account.

Learner applications are available to the parent who holds the application link and to staff of the school named on that application. They are not shown to another school.

## Principal activation

Approval of a school application creates the school and a principal account only after a platform superadmin decision. The principal receives one email link. The link works once, expires after 72 hours, and is replaced if a new invitation is sent. The principal then chooses a password. The link does not grant platform-administrator access.

## Bank details

A public school search shows only whether an account exists, with the number masked. Parent and applicant emails do not contain the account number. The full number is shown only after the person presents the application link and a verification code sent to the email address already stored on that application. The code lasts 10 minutes and can be tried five times. A matching code allows access for 30 minutes. Placeholder account numbers are not treated as payable accounts.

## Proposed retention

These operating periods are not statutory POPIA deadlines:

- A pending or abandoned school application: 90 days after the last meaningful activity.
- A declined school application: 12 months after the decision.
- A withdrawn school application: 30 days after withdrawal.
- An approved school application: 90 days after the principal creates a password, and then only for duplicate sensitive details on the application. The school record and the active principal account are not removed by that step.

Nothing in this draft authorises automatic deletion. A legal hold, an investigation hold, or an open request for access, correction, or deletion keeps that application out of retention action.

## Requests

An applicant may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of the application information.
